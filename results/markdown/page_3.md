$$\begin{gathered} \\ \left(2 \sin ^{2} x_{1}+\frac{1}{\sin ^{2} x_{1}}\right)\left(2 \sin ^{2} x_{2}+\frac{1}{\sin ^{2} x_{2}}\right) \\ \\ =\left(4 \sin ^{2} x_{1} \cdot \sin ^{2} x_{2}+\frac{1}{\sin ^{2} x_{1} \cdot \sin ^{2} x_{2}}\right)+2\left(\frac{\sin ^{2} x_{1}}{\sin ^{2} x_{2}}+\frac{\sin ^{2} x_{2}}{\sin ^{2} x_{1}}\right) \\ \\ 及 x_{2}<\frac{\pi}{4}, 则 \sin x_{2}<\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}, 2 \sin ^{2} x_{1} \cdot \sin ^{2} x_{2}<1 . \\ \end{gathered}$$

又因在区间 $[0,1]$ 上, 函数 $g(t)=t+\frac{1}{t}$ 严格递减, 故有 $$\left(2 \sin ^{2} x_{1}+\frac{1}{\sin ^{2} x_{1}}\right)\left(2 \sin ^{2} x_{2}+\frac{1}{\sin ^{2} x_{2}}\right)$$ $$>2\left(2 \sin ^{2} x_{1}^{\prime} \cdot \sin ^{2} x_{2}^{\prime}+\frac{1}{2 \sin ^{2} x_{1}^{\prime} \cdot \sin ^{2} x_{2}^{\prime}}\right)+2\left(\frac{\sin ^{2} x_{1}^{\prime}}{\sin ^{2} x_{2}^{\prime}}+\frac{\sin ^{2} x_{2}^{\prime}}{\sin ^{2} x_{1}^{\prime}}\right)$$ 从而有 $f\left(x_{1}, x_{2}, x_{3}, x_{4}\right)>f\left(x_{1}^{\prime}, x_{2}^{\prime}, x_{3}^{\prime}, x_{4}^{\prime}\right)$.

如果$x_{2}',x_{3}',x_{4}'$不全相等,则又可仿上调整而证得:当$x_{1},x_{2},x_{3},x_{4}$不全相等时,总有 $$f(x_{1},x_{2},x_{3},x_{4})>f(\frac{π}{4},\frac{π}{4},\frac{π}{4},\frac{π}{4}).$$ 可见,所求的最小值为$f(\frac{π}{4},\frac{π}{4},\frac{π}{4},\frac{π}{4})=81$,当且仅当$x_{1}=x_{2}=x_{3}=x_{4}=\frac{π}{4}$时取得.
